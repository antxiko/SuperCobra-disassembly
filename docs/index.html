Eight kilobytes in which the main thread does nothing: it starts up, hooks the interrupt and settles into a jr $ forever. The whole game fits inside the video hook, split into fifteen states and eight jobs that take turns, one frame each.

There are two kinds of picture here and they are not mixed. The tiles, the sprite drawings and the Konami logo are reconstructed: they are drawn by a tool that repeats, instruction by instruction, the cartridge's two drawing interpreters over an empty video memory. The screens are openMSX screenshots, and their captions say so; the title above is cropped from one of them. The listing and the numbers come from the binary and are reproducible with make.
